Who We Are

Ministry on Campus

The National University-Manila Ministry on Campus exists to accompany students and the university community through faith, service, formation, fellowship, and meaningful encounters.

Our Mission

To cultivate a welcoming campus community where students can grow in faith, discover purpose, serve others, and build friendships rooted in compassion and hope.

What We Do

We preserve and share ministry resources, celebrate campus activities, support formation, and document the moments that shape the community.
